# Ticket 72: store art and screenshots

Acceptance (plan): *Captures match the shipped game and supported languages.*

Plan rules that bind every item below: the store video demonstrates an **actual build**; do not show
V2 kingdoms or V3 four-player modes as current gameplay; do not advertise automation as evidence of
stability; screenshots and descriptions represent the available languages and the current feature
set. The text side is linted by `AstraKingdoms.Release store-text-lint` (see
`release/store/`).

| Field | Brief |
| --- | --- |
