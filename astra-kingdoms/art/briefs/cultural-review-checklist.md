# Cultural review checklist (first public-art gate)

Plan ("Art and sound direction"): *Use original Indian-epic fantasy architecture, textiles, materials
and formation motifs with clear silhouettes. The characters are original mortal or fictional
fantasy archers. Gods, revered figures and recognisable copyrighted characters are not fighters or
targets. Review the working game and Forge names before public branding. A cultural review covers
names, symbols, costumes and marketing language at the first public-art gate.*

Run this checklist for every new or changed public-facing asset or text (store listing, icon,
screenshot, trailer, outfit, arena, weapon or card name). Record the result in the release
decision record (`release/operations/release-decision-record-template.md`). **Reviewer(s) must
include at least one person from the communities whose traditions are drawn on**, independent of
the developer. Owner: product owner. Status today: **not run** (no production art exists).

## A. Fighters and targets

- [ ] Every fighter is an original mortal or fictional archer; none is, depicts or is named after a
      god, goddess, avatar, saint, guru, prophet or other revered figure.
- [ ] No fighter is a recognisable copyrighted character or a real person (living or historical).
- [ ] No revered figure appears as a target, victim, defeated pose, enemy or loot.
- [ ] Victory/defeat animations do not mock or desecrate any religious posture or gesture
      (e.g. namaste or prayer poses are not used as taunts).

## B. Names

- [ ] Element names (Agni, Vayu, Prithvi, Vidyut, Varuna) are presented as **elements** (fire, wind,
      earth, lightning, water), not as deities; no personification, faces or worship imagery.
      *Flag for review: Agni and Varuna are also names of Vedic deities; reviewers decide whether the
      element framing is acceptable or whether descriptive names are safer.*
- [ ] Card names (Chakra, Garuda, Suchi, Makara, Padma, Vajra) are formation shapes. *Flag: Garuda,
      Makara, Padma and Vajra carry religious meaning in several traditions; reviewers confirm the
      geometric use is respectful, and that card art shows only the envelope shape.*
- [ ] "Brahmastra" (gated experiment) is not used in public branding until reviewed.
- [ ] The game title "Astra Kingdoms" and the Forge product name have been searched for trademarks
      and community sensitivity before public branding (plan).
- [ ] Hindi and Kannada spellings and transliterations follow `glossary.txt` and were reviewed by
      fluent speakers.

## C. Symbols

- [ ] No sacred symbols as decoration, targets, cosmetics or currency: Om, swastika, trishul,
      chakra wheel of a specific deity, shivling, tilak/bindi marks as costume decoration,
      sacred thread, religious flags, scripture text.
- [ ] No national emblems, flags, regimental or political party symbols.
- [ ] The Padma card and Lotus banner use generic geometric petals, not a lotus throne or deity seat.
- [ ] Arenas contain no recognisable temple, mosque, church, gurdwara, shrine or real monument; the
      Riverside "shrine" placeholder is replaced by a neutral pavilion; no cremation or ritual scenes.
- [ ] Fire, water and river effects do not reference sacred fire (havan) or sacred-river imagery.

## D. Costumes

- [ ] Outfits are original fantasy costumes; no exact replicas of a community's ceremonial,
      religious or caste-associated dress.
- [ ] No caste, community or regional stereotypes in costume, skin tone, body type or role
      (e.g. paid outfits are not tied to a particular community).
- [ ] Skin tones are varied and none is associated with "evil", "defeat" or a price tier.
- [ ] Jewellery and headwear are generic; no religious headwear (turbans of specific faith
      significance, sacred markings) as purchasable cosmetics.

## E. Marketing language and store pages

- [ ] Descriptions call the setting "Indian-epic-inspired fantasy" and do not claim historical or
      religious accuracy or endorsement.
- [ ] No language implying the player fights, defeats or controls gods.
- [ ] No four-player, kingdom/world or V2+ promises (`store-text-lint` enforces the feature claims).
- [ ] Hindi and Kannada store text reviewed by fluent speakers for tone and unintended meanings.
- [ ] Trailer music and voice contain no devotional content.
