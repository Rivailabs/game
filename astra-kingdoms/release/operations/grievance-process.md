## 2. Intake

Channels: in-app link to the web form, e-mail, post. Each grievance gets a reference
`GR-YYYY-####` and is acknowledged with it.

Record (minimum fields, no more personal data than needed):
reference, received (UTC), channel, account or match reference if given, category (account,
purchase/refund, match result/fair play, content/conduct, data/privacy, other), description,
requested outcome.

## 4. Records and reporting

- Keep an outcome record per grievance (decision, reasons, date, operator). Proposed retention
  365 days (data map row 13) - **legal review**.
- Monthly summary (count by category, time to resolve, open items) reviewed by the owner;
  publication/reporting obligations per the Rules - **legal review**.

## 5. Design constraints that avoid other triggers (plan)

- Free entry; no stakes, no prizes of real-world value; non-transferable cosmetic recognition only.
- Do not market as officially determined or registered (Rules 8 and 12) unless true; check
  notifications before each public release.
