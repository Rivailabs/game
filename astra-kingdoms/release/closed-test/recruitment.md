# Closed-test recruitment (ticket 79)

## Invitation text (English; Hindi and Kannada versions need fluent review)

> **Help test Astra Kingdoms, a two-player archery and territory game for Android.**
>
> We are looking for about 20 adults in India with an Android phone (Android 6.0 or newer) to
> try an early test version for at least 14 days. A match takes about 10 minutes. You can play
> on one phone with a friend, against the computer, or online with a friend if the test build
> includes it.
>
> What we ask: stay opted in to the test for 14 days, play when you like (a few matches a week
> helps), and tell us what confused you, what broke and whether you wanted a rematch.
>
> What we collect: your Google account e-mail only to add you to the test list in Google Play,
> your answers in the feedback form, and the game's crash and diagnostic data described in the
> consent form. No payment is needed; test purchases are not charged. There is no prize or pay
> for taking part.
>
> To join, reply with the Google account e-mail you use on your phone and your phone model.
> You will receive the consent form first.

## Screening and coverage targets

| Need | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Testers who stay opted in 14 days | >= 12 (recruit about 20) |
| 2 GB RAM class phones | >= 3 |
| Android versions | at least three major versions incl. the minimum supported |
| Screen sizes | small (<= 6.0") and large (>= 6.5") |
| Languages | English; Hindi and Kannada readers once those builds ship |
| Cohort mix | record friend / colleague / contributor / community / recruited for every tester |
| Age | 18+ for this closed test (child flows are not yet verified) |

Do not offer money, gifts or in-game currency for participation; do not promise future rewards.
